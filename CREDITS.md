## Source material

Based on H. P. Lovecraft's short story *Dagon* (1919), which is in the public
domain. No material from any adaptation is used.

**An addition of our own:** the epilogue mentions Innsmouth, a town from Lovecraft's later story *The Shadow over Innsmouth* (1936), to hint at a larger world. It is a place name only.
The link is this adaptation's, and is not in *Dagon*. No text from that story is used, and the ending stands on its own without knowing it.
