# story: an essential moment, which nothing optional may delay or cover. optional: what the player chose to examine.

# The prologue, before the Shore. New Game only: Continue never comes here.
prologue.raider|story|The war was two years old when a raider took our ship. They kept us below, and fed us when they remembered.
prologue.escape|story|One night the watch was careless. I took a boat, a little water and some bread, and rowed until their lights were gone.
prologue.drift|story|After that there was only the sea, and the sun, and no land in any direction. I stopped counting the days.
prologue.dry|story|I slept at last. When I woke, the sea had gone.

# The Shore
shore.boat|optional|My boat lies on bare seabed. The sea has drawn back farther than any tide.
shore.fish|optional|Dead fish, rotting where the water left them. They have not been dry for long.
shore.tentacle|optional|Part of an arm, like a squid's but thicker than my leg, every sucker ringed with hooks. It is cold, and it has not begun to rot.
shore.talisman|optional|Something small, half sunk in the mud. An eye, cut above a line of waves. It is heavier than it should be, and someone wore it smooth.

# The cave, and what can be seen from its far end
cave.carving|optional|The same eye. The same waves, cut deep into the rock, and an arm coiled round them, each sucker cut with care. It isn't worn. Someone has kept it clean.
abyss.ritual|story|Figures, far off among the stones. Kneeling, all of them. All but one.

# The Exposed Abyss
abyss.fossil_a|optional|A spine as long as the boat, with too many ribs. Nothing in any sea I know was built like this.
abyss.tablet_wave|optional|A slab, cut with rising lines. "The waters rose first..."
abyss.fossil_b|optional|A skull, under the great ribs. The jaw is wider than I am tall. It faces inland, the way I am walking.
abyss.slab|optional|A slab, balanced across the gap on a single stone. Its near end has sunk into the hollow, and the far end hangs in the air.
abyss.crossing|story|It tipped under me, and came down level with a sound I felt in my teeth.
abyss.tablet_kneel|optional|A second slab. A figure, bent double. "...and they bowed..."
abyss.footprint|optional|A print in the mud, still wet at the edges. Webbed. I could lie down inside it.
abyss.relief|optional|Down here, out of the wind, a long procession cut into the rock. They are all walking toward the water, and something reaches up out of it to meet them.
abyss.cairn|optional|Stones stacked with care, looking back the way I came. Someone has sat here, and watched.
abyss.watcher|story|Upright, on the ridge. I looked again, and there was only the ridge.
abyss.tablet_eye|optional|A third slab. An open eye. "...and He saw them."

# The Monolith
canyon.statue|optional|A stone figure on its knees, hands raised, its head all wrong. It is looking at something far larger than I am.
monolith.first|story|The canyon opens, and then there is only that. I do not know who could have raised it.
mural.before|optional|Worn carvings, broken by the years. Water, perhaps. A bent shape. Something round. Ruins, and nothing more.
mural.after|optional|Lit now, and whole. Rows of figures, bowing to a vast swimmer rising from the water, its arms reaching for them, and over it, that eye. These were never ruins.
story.awakened|story|The stones answered. Somewhere behind the rock, something gave way.

# The Witness
story.reveal|story|It was no ridge. It had been beside me the whole way.
story.recoil|story|I could not breathe. I could not look away.
story.run|story|Its head turned. I ran.
flee.back|story|Don't look back. Don't...
flee.sea|story|The sea. The sea is coming back.

# The epilogue
ending.found|story|I do not remember reaching the boat. A steamer found me in it, they say, adrift and raving, days from any land. They tell me the sea never left. There was no shore, no stone, no tide drawn back.
ending.listen|story|I have stopped arguing. At night I listen to the water, and what moves under it.
ending.innsmouth|story|Years later, in a fishing town called Innsmouth, I saw a bracelet in a shop window. That eye, above those waves. The man behind the counter asked how far out I had been. I did not tell him. He did not seem to need telling.
ending.rising|story|Those figures bowed for a reason. Whatever rose that night did not rise for me, and I do not think it has finished rising.
