# Narration samples: source, rights and status

**Superseded on 2026-11-09.** The owner rejected this voice. Its three files now sit in [rejected-am_michael/](rejected-am_michael/), out of the game, and the replacement audition is in [voice-audition/](../voice-audition/README.md). What follows is the record as it was.

**Status: three samples, retained for voice direction. The owner has heard them and says the narration sounds noticeably synthetic. It is not an approved final voice, and not final narration.** ChatGPT inspected video frames only, and has not listened to the audio. **No more lines will be generated with this voice.** The game's narration integration and text fallback stay, whatever voice is chosen. The other lines of the script stay as text until the owner accepts this direction, as they asked. **The developer cannot hear audio.** Everything below is measurement and reasoning. Naturalness, clarity and emotional fit can only be judged by listening, in the game.

## How they were made

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tool** | `tools/audio/make_narration.py`, which reads `project/narration/script.txt`, the same file the game shows. It will only make lines named on its command line |
| **Model** | [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) v1.0, run locally through [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx) 0.4.9. Nothing was purchased, and no service was used |
| **Model files** | `kokoro-v1.0.onnx` SHA-256 `7d5df8ecf7d4b1878015a32686053fd0eebe2bc377234608764cc0ef3636a6c5`, `voices-v1.0.bin` SHA-256 `bca610b8308e8d99f32e6fe4197e7ec01679264efed0cac9140fe9c29f1fbf7d`. Not in the repository |
| **Voice** | `am_michael`, American English male. Chosen because Kokoro's own [voice table](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/VOICES.md) gives it the top grade among its male voices (C+), with hours of training data rather than minutes. **Not chosen by ear.** A different voice is one line in the tool |
| **Direction** | Each phrase is spoken separately, at speed 0.92, and the last at 0.88, with silence added after each (0.28 s at a full stop, 0.04 s at a comma, on top of about half a second the model leaves). A plainer, slower delivery than the model's default, for a tired man thinking as he goes. **This model has no control over emotion.** Pacing and phrasing are all that can be directed |
| **Level** | Each line set to the same loudness where he is speaking, with peaks held below -3 dB. No effects, room or processing |

**Measured:**

| Line | Length | Voiced level | Peak | Clipped samples | Speaking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `shore.talisman` | 10.68 s | -23.6 dB | -4.4 dB | 0 | 71 % |

That is about 12 characters a second, on the slow side of ordinary narration. The first attempt was slower, at 11, and its first line came out 1.6 dB quieter than the others, which is why the direction and level were changed. None of this says whether it sounds good.

## Rights, as far as they can be checked

- **License:** the [model card](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) declares Apache-2.0 for the weights, and says the model has been deployed in commercial projects. `kokoro-onnx` is MIT. Read on 2026-11-04.
- **What it was trained on,** in the model card's words: "permissive/non-copyrighted audio data", with examples of public domain audio, audio licensed under Apache, MIT and similar, and **"synthetic audio generated by closed TTS models from large providers"**.
- **For the owner to weigh before approving the rest:** that last item is the model's authors' stated position, resting on US Copyright Office guidance they cite. Whether such audio meets those providers' terms is not something I can settle. The model card does not say which recordings `am_michael` comes from, so **I cannot state that the voice resembles no identifiable person.** Nothing was done to imitate anyone: no reference recording was given, and the voice is one of the model's stock ones.
- **Disclosure:** the voice is AI-generated, and is recorded as such in `CREDITS.md` and [jam.md](../../../jam.md) §5. It must be declared on the submission form, and never described as a performance by a person.

**If the owner rejects this voice:** other Kokoro voices, or a different model, are a small change to the tool, and nothing in the game depends on which voice it is. A purchased service or a human performance needs the owner's approval first, as they said.

## Limits

- Every run of the tests and the route uses a dummy audio driver, so **no automated check has ever produced sound from these files.**
- This M3 mix has only the voice. Music, ambience and effects arrive in M4 and M5, so nothing here shows whether speech stays clear over them. The ducking on those buses is prepared and unheard.
