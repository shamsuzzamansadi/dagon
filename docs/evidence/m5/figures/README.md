# The far figures

The roadmap's art list, item 7: "the ritual and the watcher, as more than silhouettes." D111.

**[before-after.png](before-after.png):** on the left of each pair `4e48a97`, before; on the right, after. Top: the ritual as its figures kneel, and as the one who stands moves off (enlarged twice). Bottom: the watcher on the far cliff beside the moon; and the shelf in the canyon.

Each drawing is a child of the flat shape the background clips already show, move and fade, so the bowing, the watcher's dropping out of sight and the distant shape's shift are the same clips' as before. The shapes stay and draw nothing. `test_every_shape_a_clip_moves_or_fades_draws_something` holds that each carries a shown drawing.

They are a few dozen pixels each, far off, and on screen for seconds. **Looked at by the developer only.**
