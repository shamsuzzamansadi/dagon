# The motion pass after the owner's playtest of `ea86ae9`

**Uninterrupted clips with the game's own sound,** recorded from the game by Godot's Movie Maker on a private display as lossless frames at the game's 480 × 270 and its sound, at 30 frames a second, normal speed. They are scaled by exactly three, nearest pixel, to 1440 × 810, about the size the owner plays at. **Before** is `ea86ae9`, the candidate the owner played; **after** is the new candidate, `b7303bf`, whose game is `050ec57`'s (the same fingerprint; `b7303bf` changes only the art check). The takes are scripted (`project/tests/record_visual.gd`, scenes `emergence`, `escape-run` and `escape-caught`), not a person playing, and **nobody has listened to them.** The [record](../owner-playtest-2026-11-19-ea86ae9.md) has what the owner asked and what was done.

Each pair is cut from the same traced frames of its take, so a time in one is the same moment in the other.

## Measured on the takes

- **The clips' "after" is the candidate itself.** Its takes match the first candidate's, `d8626f7`, pixel for pixel, but for the falling drops of the reveal's four sprays from 2.6 to 12.3 s, whose random pattern the unseen particle drawn from the start has shifted; frames before then, with the unseen warm-up in view, are identical.
- **Every stage mark falls on the same frame in both builds** (the reveal at frame 90, its 1.5, 3.0, 4.3, 5.5, 7.0 and 9.0 s marks at 135, 181, 220, 256, 301 and 361; control at 510; the collapse at 808 running and 642 caught; the clip's end at 987 and 821), and **the sound is identical sample for sample** over each whole take, before and after.
- **The waterline.** A take of the emergence with each part drawn in a flat colour of its own and nothing else (`emergence-parts`; `~/work/dagon-m5/notes/owner-1119c/below.py`): **before, some of the creature showed below the waterline in all 271 frames, most 1724 px at 3.6 s into the reveal, past the walkway's end; after, in none.**
- **Stalls, at the game's own pace** (`record_visual.gd`'s `emergence-timing`, with no shader cached; `~/work/dagon-m5/bin/timing.sh`). Movie Maker steps the game a fixed 1/30 s whatever a frame costs, so a stall cannot show in the clips above; in play, after a long frame the clip jumps ahead. `ea86ae9`: one long frame, about 178 ms, at 2.6 s, the first spray drawn, in each of three runs. The first candidate of this pass, `d8626f7`: that one and another, about 185 ms, at 3.0 s, its shader's first draw. **Now, with the shader and a particle drawn unseen from the level's start: no frame over 100 ms in the reveal's first 7 s, in each of two runs.** Measured on this machine's software renderer; on a graphics card the stalls would be shorter, by how much was not measured.
- **The arm.** Before, the arm's square end met the body only along its lower corner in every frame from 5.5 s, 19 to 36 px of edge touching, with sky in the notch above it; the moon's rim lit the cut from 6 s. After, `tools/art/check_art.py` holds that at 73 turns across the clip's whole turn every pixel of the arm over the body lies under the shoulder, and a test holds the same in the engine at 5.6, 6.5, 6.95, 7.0 and 10.0 s.
