## The end of the artwork

The artwork disappears after the cliff opening. This is the documented boundary between the illustrated
Shore and the unfinished greybox, not a new defect, and the owner understands it as that. It stays an
explicit limitation until the later environments arrive through the planned gameplay and art milestones,
in the accepted Shore style. No new art redesign is requested
([shore-preview.md](../../shore-preview.md), [roadmap](../../roadmap.md)).
