# Five questions (send, after playing)

Please answer in your own words, straight after playing. Short answers are fine.

1. **What happened in the story,** from the beginning to the end, as you understood it?
2. **Was there a moment you weren't sure what to do or where to go?** Where was it, and what did you try?
3. **Was anything hard to see, read or hear?** Include any spoken line you didn't catch, and roughly where it was.
4. **How long did the game feel to you,** and did any part drag or feel rushed?
5. **What stayed with you most,** and was there anything that felt broken or unfinished?

## About your setup

- Windows or Linux?
- Keyboard, or a controller (which one)?
- Headphones or speakers?
- The game in a window, or full screen? Your screen's size, if you know it.
- Had you seen or heard anything about this game before?
