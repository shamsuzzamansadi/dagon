# M6 listening kit: the reveal, by ear

For the owner (M6 plan, item 3): **is each of the reveal's three spoken lines understood in the full mix,** on headphones and on speakers, above all the third, which comes over the creature's second call. Open from M5 ([acceptance record](../../evidence/m5/owner-acceptance-2026-11-20.md)).

**Please do step 1 before opening anything else in this folder.** The words are in step 2 only, so that what you report is what you heard, not what you read.

## Step 1: listen, and note what you understood

1. **[1-reveal-full-mix.wav](1-reveal-full-mix.wav), 17.6 s.** The game's own mix of the walk into the reveal and the whole reveal, as it plays: music, effects and voice together, nothing added or taken away. Optional, for context, the same moment as a video with the captions hidden: [1-reveal-full-mix-no-captions.mp4](1-reveal-full-mix-no-captions.mp4).
2. Listen **once on headphones and once on speakers,** at the volume you would play at. It is fine to listen more than once; please note how many times.
3. Fill in the [listening sheet](listening-sheet.md): what you understood of each line, in your own words, and how easily.
4. Then send the sheet, as it is, before step 2.

**What it is:** recorded from the game at `main`'s `a4250f0`, the same game as the accepted M5 build and the playtest baseline, by Godot's Movie Maker on a private display (scripted, not played), from the last checkpoint. The game writes 32-bit samples; this file is the same at 16 bits so that any player opens it; its peak is 1.3 dB below full scale, so nothing clips. Nobody has listened to it before you; the developer cannot hear.

## Step 2: only after reporting

The folder [after-you-report](after-you-report/README.md) holds the three lines' words with their times, and the voice alone, with the music and effects muted. Comparing what you heard with the words is the point of the second step; the voice alone shows what the line itself sounds like without what plays beneath it.
